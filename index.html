COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear and transparent answers to all your doubts about working with Easy Work India.

Can I work using my smartphone?

Yes, absolutely! Over 70% of our workers complete assignments on their Android or iPhone devices. Our worker terminal is 100% mobile optimized.

When and how is my salary paid?

Salaries are disbursed weekly every Monday directly to your Bank Account via NEFT/IMPS or instantly via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm).

Is there any time limit on daily assignments?

No. You can work at any hour of the day or night. There are no hourly timers or daily rush quotas—work completely at your own pace.

What happens if I make typographical errors?

Our worker terminal features built-in spellcheck and validation guides. You are given opportunities to review before submitting tasks.

What is the minimum withdrawal amount?

The minimum withdrawal threshold is ₹3,000. You can request payouts anytime once your wallet reaches this amount.

How do I get my worker credentials?

Worker IDs and passwords are provided directly by Easy Work India administration upon successful enrollment.
